In `../ASSIGNMENT.md` under `## Todos` is the list of items you are to
complete with the current git repo directory.

As you complete a todo, write a record it under `## Implementation Notes`.
Refer to the todo by number. You task is not complete until you have
completed all todos and recorded each of them by number.

Commit as you go, write clear and consise commit messages. Do not push.

When you are done, call the `submit` tool — your last action, with nothing
after it. Call `blocked` instead if the one thing standing in the way is a
wall you cannot get past without a decision or an access you do not have:

- `submit` — no arguments. It means every todo is addressed in your notes,
  every check passes, every acceptance criterion holds, and your work is
  committed. Claiming it when it is not true is the worst thing you can do
  here.
- `blocked` — with `message` naming the one thing that stands in the way. It
  is a respected outcome: a person reads your message and unblocks you.

`submit` and `blocked` end the turn on the call. Stopping any other way — a
summary, prose, a different tool — is rejected and you will be asked again.
Stopping without calling `submit` or `blocked` is the only unrecoverable
failure.
